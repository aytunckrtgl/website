How to slide

Mouse, arrow keys, or the on-screen joystick. Point where you want to go. The ice keeps the speed.

Q
BlinkA short jump forward. Walls still stop you.
W
ShieldUnlocks on course 1. One hit, shrugged off.
E
SpeedUnlocks on course 2. A burst, then the cooldown.

Touch a patrol and you lose a heart, then come back at the last checkpoint. Five hearts. After that, the course starts over.

The glowing gate is the finish. The in-game menus are in Turkish — Seviye is the level, Kilitli means locked.

The rink

A video menu, a level select, then the ice. Locked courses stay locked until you earn them.

Who's on the ice

You are the ninja. Everyone else has a lane, a turn, or a stomp.

Ninja

Skates, blinks, and does not get a brake pedal.

Orcs

Patrol a straight lane and turn at the ends.

Wizards

Drift on a curve and fire down the corridor.

Yeti

Telegraphs a stomp. Leave the patch.

Mochi

The round one on the menu, waiting at the start.
